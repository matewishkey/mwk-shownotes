# After the show — paste this into the agent on your computer

Mate gives you this off camera, once the recording has stopped. It is the only thing you paste.

```
I have just recorded a Mate Wish Key show on this
computer, and Mate has asked me to send him the notes
for it.

Read COLLECT.md in this public repo and do exactly
what it says, in order:

  https://github.com/matewishkey/mwk-shownotes

It looks at the last six hours on this machine and
nothing older. You show me everything before any of it
leaves, project by project, and nothing is sent until
I say yes.
```

## What it does

It reads the conversations from the last six hours, takes out anything that should not travel,
writes up what you built — one file per project, kept separate — and shows you the lot.

Then it asks. You can drop any project by name, or say no and nothing goes anywhere.

If you say yes it puts the notes in an unlisted gist on your own GitHub account and gives you the
link to send to Mate. **The gist is yours** — you can read it, change it or delete it at any time.

## What never leaves

Your files, the commands the agent ran, and anything that looked like a password, a key or a token.
It keeps what you asked for and what the agent answered, and nothing else.
